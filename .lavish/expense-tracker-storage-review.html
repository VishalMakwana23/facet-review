Decision 01 · expense tracker foundation

Where should the ledger live?

Choose the first storage model for a personal expense tracker. The decision should protect sensitive financial information, support dependable recovery, and leave room for a second device later.

Decision context

One primary user. Financial data is sensitive. Seamless phone-and-laptop access is mandatory at launch. Reliable recovery still matters, so sync must be paired with an independent export path.

Benefits by approach

Both can work; they optimize for different kinds of confidence.

Local-only storage

A private ledger stored on the device, with deliberate export and backup.

  • Smallest privacy and operational footprint.
  • Works offline and avoids account setup.
  • Fastest path to a useful first release.
  • Clear ownership of the raw data and file format.

Cloud synchronization

An account-backed ledger replicated between approved devices.

  • Phone and laptop can share one current ledger.
  • Recovery is less dependent on one physical device.
  • Automatic backups can reduce manual discipline.
  • Creates a foundation for future sharing or integrations.

Risks and mitigations

The recommendation depends less on whether a risk exists than on whether it is containable at launch.

Local-only storage

Risk
Device loss or disk failure can make the ledger unrecoverable.
Mitigate
Add encrypted, user-triggered exports; document a backup cadence; make restore a tested workflow.
Risk
It cannot provide seamless access to one current ledger on both phone and laptop at launch.
Mitigate
Manual export/import is only a fallback; meeting the requirement would require adding synchronization, so local-only is not a viable launch choice under the new constraint.

Cloud synchronization

Risk
Sensitive financial data gains a server, account, and credential attack surface.
Mitigate
Use encryption in transit and at rest, strong authentication, minimal retention, and a clear export/delete path.
Risk
Offline edits can conflict or become dependent on service availability.
Mitigate
Define offline behavior, conflict rules, retries, and an independent export before launch.

Recommended launch path

Launch with cloud sync, guarded by strong privacy and recovery controls.

Seamless phone-and-laptop access is now mandatory, so local-only storage no longer meets the launch goal. Cloud synchronization is the direct fit, despite its larger security and operational surface. Make encryption, authentication, conflict handling, offline behavior, and an independent export path launch requirements—not follow-up work.

Why this wins now
The mandatory access requirement outweighs the local-only simplicity advantage; safeguards contain the added privacy and reliability risks.
