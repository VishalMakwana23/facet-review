## What is stronger than the historical baseline

Facet now combines a polished decision canvas with workflow depth: stable anchored comments, threaded review, revision history, typed decisions, focused lenses, semantic visual evidence, offline-first local storage, compact patches, and host-neutral automation. Visuals and views derive from one artifact, so changing lenses does not require regenerating content.

Plans now use a dedicated milestone-path recipe. The first viewport states scope, phase count, risk count, and decision gate; architecture is explained as a small system flow with detailed modules disclosed on demand; repeated risks and acceptance criteria are grouped for scanning; and the recommendation leads directly into the final decision surface. Section navigation is an on-demand overlay, preserving canvas width instead of permanently taxing every report.

Token efficiency comes from short presentation-free intents, deterministic local compilation, compact feedback digests, and surgical patches. The token receipt labels character-derived estimates honestly; it does not represent Codex billing usage.

## Competitive claim boundary

Engineering gates and automated accessibility evidence are not a substitute for blind human preference. The frozen five-case Facet-versus-Lavish harness remains fail-closed until all matched captures, content-parity checks, task results, authoritative usage where available, and blind votes are complete. Until then, describe implemented capabilities and measured machine results, not overall competitive superiority.
